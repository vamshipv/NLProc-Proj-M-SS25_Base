Winnie the Pooh is a beloved children's book character created by A. A. Milne.
The stories revolve around Pooh Bear and his friends Piglet, Tigger, Eeyore, and others in the Hundred Acre Wood.
The character first appeared in 1926 and has since become a cultural icon.
Some people prefer reading the original books over watching animated adaptations.
Books are often seen as more detailed than movies.
Pooh Bear's simple, kind nature teaches valuable lessons about friendship and kindness.
